The question

The network already exists.

After GSA, life unfolds again across countries, time zones, generations and different paths.

Belonging can remain alive. Meeting each other, not always.

The thesis

We don't want to create a new community.

We want to care for the one that already exists.

What's missing
It's not a lack of interesting people.
It's not a lack of network.
It's not a lack of desire.What's missing is space for the encounter to happen.
The ritual

Small enough to fit into life.
Meaningful enough to keep a thread alive.

What actually grows
3

possible new threads in a single circle

A conversation among three people can create three new relational pairs. Over time, the network memory begins to reveal gaps, repetitions and bridges not yet made.

We don't want to maximize matches. We want to increase the threads in the network.

Start small

The first version only needs to be good enough for the first circle to happen.

Round. Fixed time slots. A draw with memory. Conversation link. Calendar. Confirmation. No feed, chat, rich profiles, gamification or another platform to maintain.
